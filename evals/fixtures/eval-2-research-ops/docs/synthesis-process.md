# How we synthesize research

The process we (try to) follow between a finished study and a published
insight report:

1. **Tag raw notes.** Each interview or survey response gets tagged against
   `tagging-taxonomy.md` as it comes in — while it's fresh, not batched at
   the end.
2. **Affinity map.** Cluster tagged notes in FigJam by theme. This is where
   cross-participant patterns become visible instead of just a pile of
   individual comments.
3. **Draft against the template.** Every finding in the draft needs a
   verbatim quote or direct observation attached — a finding without
   evidence doesn't go in the report, it goes in open questions instead.
4. **Peer review.** Another researcher reads the draft before it's shared
   wider, checking specifically that confidence ratings match how many
   participants actually raised each point.
5. **Publish.** Report goes in `examples/` (or the live drive equivalent)
   for the team.

The gap between our two example reports is exactly steps 3-4 breaking down
under time pressure — the checkout report went through the full process, the
onboarding one was written same-day for a stakeholder ask and skipped peer
review entirely. We know the difference and we're not proud of the second
one; it's here as an example of what happens when synthesis gets rushed.
