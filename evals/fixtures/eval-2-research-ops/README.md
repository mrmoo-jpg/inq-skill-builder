# Wayfinding Research — shared folder

This is the shared research-ops folder for the Wayfinding team (product research
at Lumen Health, a fictional health app). It's the working area three
researchers use day to day.

- `templates/` — the report format we standardize on
- `examples/` — past insight reports, kept as reference for new team members
- `docs/` — how we actually run synthesis, plus our tag vocabulary
- `raw-notes/` — unprocessed interview transcripts (participant-identifying —
  handle with care, not for casual reading or copy-paste)
- `admin/` — team operations stuff, unrelated to the research workflow itself

We're drowning a bit in inconsistent report quality between researchers and
want something that turns raw interview notes into a report that actually
matches our template and our bar for evidence.
