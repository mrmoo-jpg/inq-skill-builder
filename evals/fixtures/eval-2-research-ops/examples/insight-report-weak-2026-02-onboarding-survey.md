# Onboarding survey notes

Ran a survey about onboarding in Feb. Here's what people said, roughly.

Sample was decent sized. Mostly people said onboarding was fine but a few
people had trouble with the notifications permission screen, it seemed
confusing to some users. Also some people mentioned the account setup step
felt long, not totally clear why.

Overall onboarding seems mostly ok, might be worth revisiting the
notifications screen at some point if there's time. Could also look at
account setup length.

Will share more if we do a follow-up.
