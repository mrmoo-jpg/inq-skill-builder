# Title
Users abandon checkout when shipping cost appears after they've already committed

## Date
2026-04

## Research question
Why did checkout completion drop 9 points after the March release?

## Method
6 moderated usability tests, existing-customer panel, think-aloud protocol,
30 minutes each.

## Participants
P01–P06 (existing Lumen Health subscribers, mixed device: 4 mobile, 2 desktop)

## Key findings

1. **Finding:** Shipping cost revealed only at the final review step reads as
   a bait-and-switch and causes last-second abandonment.
   **Evidence:** "Wait, it wasn't this much a second ago — I feel like I'm
   being tricked" (P04, mobile). P03 and P05 independently closed the tab at
   this exact step without completing.
   **Confidence:** High (5 of 6 participants hesitated or dropped at this
   step)
   **Recommendation:** Surface estimated shipping cost on the cart page,
   before the final review step.

2. **Finding:** The "apply promo code" field is mistaken for a required field,
   and its collapsed state hides that it's optional.
   **Evidence:** P01 spent 40 seconds searching for a promo code before
   asking "do I have to have one of these?" P03 had the same hesitation.
   **Confidence:** Medium (2 of 6 participants)
   **Recommendation:** Label the field "Promo code (optional)" and keep it
   visually de-emphasized relative to required fields.

## Themes
- Cost transparency (finding 1)
- Perceived-required fields that aren't (finding 2)

## Open questions
Does surfacing shipping estimates earlier change conversion, or just move the
same hesitation upstream? Worth an A/B test before calling this solved.
